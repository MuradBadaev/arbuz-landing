# Surface brief: landing (index.html)

Scope: single-page landing for «Арбуз», grocery shop with delivery in с. Джалган. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: village residents ordering from their phones, often in the evening. Job: see that the shop has what they need, send an order in WhatsApp.
Action: «Заказать в WhatsApp» (wa.me link; phone number pending from owner) and an on-page list builder that composes the WhatsApp message.
Proof: 19 real shop photos, Yandex Maps rating 4,5 / 18 оценок, three real public reviews. No prices, no delivery fee, no minimum order, no days of week: all «уточняйте при заказе».
Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS; Russian copy only; never invent facts.
Quality bar (user-chosen): ВкусВилл and Купер.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard of a Russian grocery-delivery site, executed at ВкусВилл/Купер craft: clean light page, big real photos, category tiles, one obvious WhatsApp action. Refuses irony, quirk, and stock imagery; the shop's own photos and watermelon brand carry the character.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, soft rind-tinted grey surfaces, near-black green-tinted ink. Watermelon red for brand marks and emphasis, rind green for every action button. Rubik in heavy weights for headings, Rubik regular for text. Large radii (20–32px), pill buttons and chips, sticker-like round badges, a watermelon-flesh CTA band (red with black seeds, rind stripe on top). Authored line icons, one stroke weight.

STORY: Visitor learns in seconds: this is «Арбуз» on Дагестанская, 12, it delivers around Джалган, it carries far more than its size suggests (real shelves prove it), and ordering is one WhatsApp message. They browse tiles, tap items into a list, and send it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky white header (logo mark + name, address, hours, green WhatsApp pill). Below: left ~46% — H1 «Привезём продукты из «Арбуза» к вашей двери», subline, green primary «Заказать в WhatsApp», secondary «Собрать список», facts row (часы, зона, рейтинг). Right — rounded photo collage: night storefront with red neon large, two interior shots smaller, a round red sticker «Доставка по Джалгану». Category tiles start just under the fold.

FORM: Canon (standing exit chosen by the user), not on the ordered grounded list; seed key e62160bc. Signature interaction: list builder — chips by category plus free input, live list with quantities, a WhatsApp-bubble preview of the exact message, one send button; mobile sticky bar shows the count and sends.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
